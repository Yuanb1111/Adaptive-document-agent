Avoid mathematically confusing negative wording:
- For expenses (R&D, Selling & Marketing, G&A, Cost of Sales, other expenses) stored as negative accounting figures (e.g. -95.9 -> -121.1): interpret expense magnitude correctly (larger absolute negative = expenses increased/rose; smaller absolute negative = expenses decreased/declined). Never say expenses decreased when absolute spending increased!
- For liabilities and deficits, use "widened" or "narrowed" (e.g. "Net current liabilities widened from RMB1.10bn to RMB1.72bn").
- For negative EBITDA / losses, state "loss narrowed" or "loss widened" (e.g. "Adjusted EBITDA loss narrowed from RMB683m to RMB25m").
- For series with net decline and intermediate recovery (e.g. 14.8% -> 7.9% -> 9.3%), describe as "declined overall, with a partial rebound in the final year/period", never "increased overall".
