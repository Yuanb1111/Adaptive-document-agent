# Bounded editorial work and budget scheduling

v147 addresses failures and repeated work at the boundaries between source
reading, editorial synthesis, independent review and physical presentation
measurement. The changes use source-owned contracts rather than issuer names,
document types, fixed page numbers or predefined analytical topics.

## Introduction compression

Layout repair now requests `SummaryLayoutPages`, a strict pages-only response.
Python retains the original identity values and their literal quotations and
page bindings. A compression cannot rewrite that protected envelope, even when
another quotation would also be valid source text.

Initial synthesis, layout compression and targeted paragraph repair return
`meaning_refs` containing only `part_id` and `meaning_index`. The model chooses
which current paragraph expresses each meaning. Python binds that paragraph's
complete exact text as the canonical `summary_quote`; it does not search old
copy, infer a mapping or grant semantic coverage. Missing, unknown, duplicate
and wrongly owned references remain subject to the existing validators.

For a selected audience introduction, the accepted `required_meanings` define
the assertion scope. Complete source passages and catalog facts provide evidence
and necessary qualification context; they are not an additional audience
checklist. The writer must preserve every selected assertion and its necessary
numbers, periods, bases, attribution, uncertainty and conditions, without adding
unrelated independent assertions from those passages. Compression may remove
incidental unselected assertions from an earlier draft only when they are not
necessary qualifiers of selected meaning. All original fact IDs, source
provenance and identity remain protected. Without an audience selection, the
existing complete-source mode retains its all-authored-claims protection.

The initial author receives the native template's physical capacity. Subsequent
repairs receive current copy, complete owned source quotations, every required
meaning, per-group physical page counts, and measured full-width and native-grid
loads. The feedback distinguishes a group starting a new slide from a
continuation using another slot. Unpaginated group costs expose the exact
one-slot excess, wrapped-line savings, title/label overhead and paragraph gaps;
spare space on already split slides cannot be pooled across group boundaries.
Optional numeric paragraph-merger alternatives retain only existing item
addresses and measured costs. They do not change copy or establish coherent
meaning; the model must retain meaning carried by labels as well as body text.
Models may reorganize related assertions across
groups and remove repeated wording; they may not remove distinct selected
meanings, their necessary numbers or qualifications, or source provenance.
Fonts, slide caps and semantic validators are unchanged.

Initial selected-audience drafting also removes the conflicting 160–240-character
paragraph target and below-1,200-character preference from its system guidance.
Compatible distinct meanings may share a coherent paragraph with all owning
references. The 6,000-character transport ceiling remains, legacy drafting keeps
its existing guidance, and no paragraph length grants semantic acceptance. The
effect of this cold-path prompt clarification on live latency is unverified.

The content budget remains one initial draft and at most two selected-audience
compression responses, with separately bounded format recovery. Compression
requests do not replay the full synthesis history or identity pages. Exact
source bindings and authored meaning references are checked again, followed by
mandatory fresh independent review of the complete selected meaning union after
geometry succeeds. That review still rejects a missing necessary qualifier or
an unsupported claim; incidental unselected facts are not new required content.
Physical fit and exact paragraph binding never establish semantic acceptance.

Authored meaning-reference deficits are recorded before the first compression;
the original authored copy is not assumed to contain every selected assertion.
The writer may restore an already-selected assertion from its complete owned
source while compressing. A first compression missing only required meaning
references may use the existing final compression round, starting from the
original protected identity/fact baseline with the rejected copy retained as
diagnostic context. Unknown references, wrong ownership, lost protected facts
and forbidden placements still fail. The last candidate must pass every strict
binding and physical check and fresh independent per-meaning review; adding a
reference without restoring visible meaning cannot establish coverage.

## Complete coverage and final briefing

- Introduction selection review checks the union of all assertions owned by a
  source part. It distinguishes a missing necessary qualification from a new
  independent fact that needs a section relevance reason. A late meaning
  rejection can use the existing third semantic round for one finding ledger
  and targeted correction. Every finding has an exact source binding and
  disposition; unchanged copy requires explicit already-covered references or
  an independently judged scope reason. The final full-source review must
  accept the result. Split assertions retain separate, explicitly named before
  and after ID inventories. Complete source passages occur once on the review
  wire; canonical quotations and digests remain intact in the audit.
  The budget is at most three semantic rounds. If the first rejection needs a
  selection rewrite and the second identifies meaning defects for the first
  time, a targeted first meaning correction can use the third round's fresh
  review. This does not create a fourth semantic round or another unbounded
  correction loop. A shared binding re-review only repairs a verdict's exact
  current-draft addressing; it cannot approve rejected content automatically.
  Selection and full-source importance review start with a 32,000-token output
  ceiling. Only an explicitly length-truncated response can receive the one
  capacity retry at 40,000 tokens, using the same complete source and current
  review target. Partial JSON is never accepted or used to fill missing findings.
- Topic coverage uses independent bounded delta requests over one immutable
  accepted snapshot. Each batch receives every point and qualification for its
  assigned series and decides every requested series exactly once. Concurrent
  completion order cannot change the selection. A shared final model merge
  receives complete proposed additions and retains all previously accepted
  observations. Context, decision and call limits fail explicitly; there is no
  sampling of source points to make a request fit.
  When the mandatory introduction guard fails, coverage records unresolved
  history containing the original selection, every completed delta response,
  the blocked request, cancelled batches and still-unreviewed series. Already
  admitted requests drain into that history. The exact mandatory guard failure
  is then re-raised; provisional additions and incomplete coverage never become
  an accepted selection, and cancellation never becomes an omission decision.
- A selected plan already known to exceed a slide limit can defer briefing until
  model-owned physical budget selection is stable. A successful refinement's
  independently checked briefing is reused; otherwise the stable plan receives
  one briefing and a new native budget check. Failure retains a partial reporting
  checkpoint. After a verified introduction is attached, one preliminary native
  projection can also detect physical spill missed by logical slide counts.
  Its isolated snapshot cannot mutate the live plan. An unmeasurable preliminary
  plan retains ordinary scheduling; post-visual measurement and final source,
  meaning and export gates remain mandatory. No extra conclusion reserve is
  added to this scheduling projection, since fallback summary pages can be
  replaced by the final briefing. This projection has a real CPU cost and is
  not itself a speed guarantee.
- Reporting retries supply a deep copy of the retained presentation plan to the
  existing introduction preparation gate. A complete introduction can be reused
  only after current source spans, reading completeness, audience/meaning
  digests, visible copy and physical layout pass normal validation; attachment
  validates it again. Incomplete or tampered copy, evidence or review bindings
  trigger ordinary regeneration. The caller's checkpoint is never mutated.
- Physical budget refinement returns only topics and one decision per explicit
  editable series ID. Historical omissions are read-only context outside that
  ledger; topics retain their original evidence ownership. Exact missing, extra,
  duplicate and mismatched IDs are reported without inventing a repair. The
  canonical bounded omissions field remains a display list, while the complete
  original history and every new model decision and reason stay in the budget
  audit and undergo independent source and omission review. Allocation hints use
  measured current overhead and both body/all-file caps rather than a fixed
  allowance. They cannot remove series, establish importance or replace final
  native export checks after the fresh briefing. A compiled native projection
  rejects a known over-budget proposal before paying for omission review and
  supplies exact measured feedback to the remaining existing proposal round.
  The decision ledger precedes rewritten topics, and feedback names the actual
  subset-only actions, required physical reduction and measured topic costs.
  Copy-only rewrites cannot be mistaken for omitted chart evidence. Measured
  native groups expose the planned slide, chart/matrix roles,
  actual physical pages and each original series' complete/partial renderer-input
  membership. Removing one row from a retained matrix does not establish a page
  saving. This provenance is not claim-by-claim visible coverage or a simulation
  of how a proposed subset will fit; only the next native measurement proves it.
  Within one invocation, an exact full compiled-input and template-revision match can reuse
  an already failed projection instead of building the same rejected deck again;
  compilation and source validation still run. No fitting result, review verdict
  or acceptance is cached. Projections add no speculative Conclusion pages to
  fallback summary content that the new briefing replaces.
  Every fitting candidate still needs fresh independent review of its exact
  compiled source scope before briefing or commit. Review explanations have a
  bounded 8,000-character transport capacity; a longer explanation neither grants
  acceptance nor creates another format or semantic correction round.

## Brief source and comparison contracts

A monetary table quote may express currency in its column headers and scale on
a separate header line. Recovery requires both literal headers in the same
continuous quoted table, the exact amount/percentage column structure, the own
column's currency and one unambiguous scale. Disjoint fragments, neighboring
tables, an unquoted declaration or a different currency column cannot lend
support. Complete row, sign, source-period and magnitude checks still apply.

An independently reviewed comparison distinguishes endpoint levels from a
literal source-reported change. Endpoint direction is calculated from its exact
source-bound values, including signed outflows and explicitly declared absolute
magnitudes. An optional echoed source-direction diagnostic does not alter that
calculation. Actual reported-change amount or reference fields cannot be mixed
into endpoint transport.

A reported-change comparison binds its exact audience claim, literal source
phrase and own source-catalog reference. Its complete currency, native scale and
coefficient must match both claim and source; a bare coefficient cannot borrow
a monetary amount elsewhere in the quotation. A directly reported percentage
change similarly retains its literal percent unit on both sides; bare numbers,
percentage points, basis points and money cannot substitute for it. No computed
rate or unit conversion enters through this transport. A BY amount requires an actual
change predicate. A shared predicate requires the complete contiguous claim,
including that predicate, rather than a detached second noun phrase. An OF
amount must belong to its own change noun in the same clause; an earlier change
statement cannot turn a later closing balance into a reported delta. These
checks do not invent missing start/end levels or choose the source metric's
meaning. Same-duration, source-direction and zero-change checks remain in force.

A meaning-only patch may interchange an already displayed compact period with
its complete duration and closing date. A complete original period supplies
that date; a compact original label requires one unique full date in its own
literal narrative or verified, completely quoted table-header geometry. Annual
and interim date groups stay separate. No calendar closing date is assumed from
a duration: for example, a nine-month period can end on November 15. Aliases are
masked symmetrically in original and proposed copy, so their duration coefficient
does not authorize a new bare count. A different date, year, duration, monetary
amount or newly enumerated table cell still needs the ordinary source checks
and cannot enter through this exception. A relative prior-year clarification
additionally requires its current and prior periods in complete quoted columns.

The reviewer receives all owned narrative and table evidence. An exact narrative
total does not need to be replaced with newly enumerated component cells.
Audit-status caveats apply only to their exact supported period and every
affected measure; a caveat on the first clause does not qualify a later measure
implicitly. A missing-status finding must identify its exact measure, period and
literal source-supported status, and reconcile that obligation with the same
source column. A qualifier already present on that measure's supported
comparative is not missing; an unmarked current period cannot require an
unsupported label. Unknown assurance remains unknown. The reviewer verdict
remains authoritative, and an unchanged rejected patch keeps its failed memo.
Meaning-patch format recovery and targeted
contract feedback share the existing two-request repair budget, and a corrected
candidate still requires fresh independent meaning review. No source or audit
qualification is fabricated to complete a patch.

The existing single residual source-correction request may explicitly select
zero-based indices of obsolete magnitude annotations to remove. Removal requires
distinct existing integer addresses, no remaining positive display using the
annotation, and the exact same negative native quantity still displayed with its
currency and scale. The model cannot add or rebind annotations through this
patch. Python performs no automatic cleanup; all other source metadata stays
locked. A signed amount needs no positive-magnitude annotation, while positive
magnitude wording still requires its exact source-bound annotation. Full source
checks and fresh independent meaning review remain mandatory after the change.

The focused briefing regression group contains 505 passing tests, including
adversarial currency/scale binding, non-change BY wording, detached predicates,
cross-clause OF wording, odd closing dates, mixed annual/interim geometry and
new-count rejection. Private retained-response checks preserve original failed
verdicts and prove mechanical source/transport behavior only; they do not create
a new semantic approval or establish a complete live-run outcome.

## Request and CPU costs

Bounded editorial synthesis and review operations use the configured reduced
reasoning policy on positively supported provider routes. The provider-default
opt-out remains available. Unknown models, capabilities and custom endpoints
retain provider defaults; all model access still crosses `LLMGateway`, including
Local Only privacy checks. Reduced reasoning does not skip source validation,
independent review, numerical checks or rejection budgets. See
[the operation policy](LLM_REASONING_POLICY.md).
The full introductory importance review retains provider reasoning defaults;
its union/scope judgment is a semantic selection gate. Physical introduction
compression also retains provider defaults because fitting the complete selected
meaning into fewer pages requires substantive synthesis. Physical-budget selection
retains provider defaults too: choosing which comparisons and complete chart groups
to retain requires a source-aware priority decision. Repeated ineffective proposals
motivated this conservative policy choice; they do not establish reduced reasoning
as the cause. The independent budget-review policy remains unchanged.

Candidate scoring uses the configured gateway concurrency budget, with one slot
reserved while a mandatory introduction worker is active. Local/stateful clients
remain serial. Initial decision batches contain 20 complete candidates, while
every request retains the same full global comparison context. This reduces
exposure to a large truncated response; singleton split recovery still applies.
It is a capacity tradeoff: without truncation, smaller batches increase input
and call cost. Synthetic benchmarks exercise both outcomes and do not establish
a live speed gain.

Each explicitly length-truncated scoring attempt can retain its exact text in
the optional `SemanticScoringBatchAudit.truncated_response` diagnostic field,
alongside the original candidate IDs, error and output-token count. Existing
gateway chunk IDs and recovery-depth metadata remain intact. Older audits read
with a null diagnostic field. The retained text is untrusted history, never a
source of accepted decisions, even if the length-finished output happens to be
valid JSON. This permits later inspection of response echo or repeated reasons
without guessing their cause from token counts or changing prompt semantics.

CPU optimizations reuse parsed geometry within one reading validation and cache
literal label/regex parsing by their complete inputs. They do not cache source
values, periods or semantic acceptance. Compact speaker-note JSON and single
OOXML assignments avoid repeated serialization while retaining audit content.
Partial returns now refresh gateway usage and wall time after final budget or
briefing failure. Deferred briefing work is included in the physical-budget
phase; overlapping detail timers must not be added as independent durations.

`2026-10-16-bounded-editorial-and-budget-scheduling-v147`, with `analysis-v76`.
Extraction remains `extraction-v20`; no raw extraction contract changes in this
update. Older affected analysis/reporting checkpoints cannot silently supply the
new contract's outputs.

outside Git. The failed introduction's saved-response replay now preserves its
identity envelope and all 16 required meaning bindings, then correctly rejects
the historical copy's 8-to-8-to-7 physical pages against a five-page cap. This is
an offline protocol regression, with no new model response or acceptance.

measured 40.47 seconds at baseline and 17.60 seconds with the changes. Visible

changed. These measurements do not establish full-pipeline latency.
