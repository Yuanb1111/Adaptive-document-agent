# Compact text pages

PPT export now folds brief/closing pages with at most two body lines and four
combined heading/body lines into an existing substantive page. Native charts,
tables, source pictures, additional independently rendered caveats, covers,
section dividers and closing artwork do not qualify as sparse text pages.

Complete copy is added to a measured free margin where possible. Otherwise the
page is omitted and its complete copy, source references and original notes
remain in the receiving page's notes. No filler is generated and no source
observations are changed. A deck without a substantive receiving page is retained.

The export rebuilds affected contents entries, renumbers package parts and
rebases physical slide numbers in source coverage records and durable export
traces. Co-located source scopes remain available in trace notes. Only the export
cache version changes; extraction and analysis results can be reused.

The analysis67 review export has 17 pages rather than 18. Its one-line review
status now appears below an existing Data Index table instead of on its own page.

The v93 follow-up preserves JSON speaker-note dictionaries when merging short
pages and rebasing export traces. Original evidence fields remain unchanged,
merged pages have their own structured field, and prior trace numbers are kept
separately from the current mapping. Plain-text notes retain their existing form.
