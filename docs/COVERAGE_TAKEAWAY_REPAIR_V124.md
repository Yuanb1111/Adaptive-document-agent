# v124: complete coverage review and recoverable takeaways

Two independent editorial warnings exposed different failures. The coverage
review repeatedly sent qualifications for every deferred series, leaving room
for only one new series per request. Its eight-batch limit could not review a
large catalog. Separately, a valid question could retain a withheld takeaway
without a final, source-scoped wording repair.

Coverage batches now carry all qualifications for their selected and requested
series. Deferred qualifications travel with their own later batch; the global
catalog remains available, and no source point or candidate is sampled away.
The review permits at most 24 batches and 36 model calls, with at most three
attempts per batch. A repeated validation error stops that batch. Distinct new
errors receive specific feedback, including the exact rejected topic fields
and available source periods. Partial reviews remain transactional and cannot
clear the incomplete-coverage warning.

Coverage is also distinguished from duplicate display. Exact corroborating
views already represented by selected evidence do not require another topic.
Matching checks retain value, raw value, period, scale, unit, currency, entity,
category, definition and audit status. If the model calls a represented view
"omitted" because no additional display is needed, its original decision remains
in the audit while the structural coverage ledger records the proven links.
An inclusion without represented source facts still fails.

Complete source-row composition panels reuse the existing composition validator
when selected alongside independent series. Multiple categories in a period
are not treated as conflicting scalar values; incomplete or unreconciled
matrices remain rejected. Topic-count schema failures receive a bounded
semantic regrouping request with the original source context and accepted
evidence constraints. They are never repaired by truncating the topic list.

Takeaway correction is separate from coverage selection and slide compilation.
The model receives the original selected question, full linked observations,
and rejection details. Only its takeaway text can change. It has two attempts,
with repeated text rejected. A newly compiled plan must pass the existing
evidence, numeric, period and directional checks without introducing another
withdrawal before the text is restored. Failed attempts and the original claim
remain in the audit. An empty takeaway withheld by earlier selection still
requires review; repairing another topic cannot erase that warning.

Topic numeric checks now recognize the signed value of complete accounting
source cells, such as `(100)`, through the existing numeric parser. This avoids
mistaking a sourced `RMB(100) thousand` claim for an invented negative value.
Source values and units remain unchanged; downstream quantity checks continue
to validate scale, currency and attribution.

The new correction uses LLMGateway and the existing configurable reduced
reasoning policy for editorial work, with a 2,000-token output limit. Local
Only protections and provider independence remain unchanged.

When an explicit body-only slide limit is exceeded, existing analytical
deep-dive continuations can move together to the appendix if their theme's
overview remains in the body. This preserves every native shape, chart,
table and source reference. Physical slide numbers and durable source mappings
are rebased after the move. Total-deck limits cannot be satisfied this way.
Stacked chart labels also reserve space for wrapped legends before estimating
segment fit; crowded labels are omitted while all values remain in editable
chart data and the source appendix.
