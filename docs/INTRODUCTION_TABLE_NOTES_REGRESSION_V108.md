## Introduction and preservation

Editorial transport accepts complete copy up to the reading-fact limit. Python
splits it into readable items, retaining every character and binding continuation
order, count, digest, source quote, page and substantive part. Validation checks
the complete claim rather than disconnected numeric fragments. The eight-page
model quota applies to each editorial response, not the assembled introduction.

After bounded editorial correction fails, the agent can lay out the already
model-authored, source-validated reading facts for that batch. It makes no new
importance decisions or analytical claims. Long literal reading extracts can
make this fallback verbose; concise new prose still depends on the editorial
model. Privacy failures and cancellation do not trigger this recovery.

Every substantive part remains mandatory in default and customized runs.
Unsupported wording stays rejected. Native binding supports distinct facts with
identical continuation text. Export cleanup cannot trim source-bound connectors,
units or annotations; complete introduction and appendix bindings are rechecked
after cleanup and serialization.

## Language and executive briefing

The translation inventory includes unit words and the source-validation limit.
Source page lists no longer treat separator commas as part of their number.
Standard citation wrappers have Chinese labels; original table cells and original
source footnotes remain literal and separate from narrative localization. Sparse
pages are removed only when their entire copy is visibly transferred elsewhere.

Translation prompts and semantic review distinguish debt/indebtedness from total
liabilities. A deterministic check rejects their known Chinese conflation while
preserving valid liabilities wording. All amount, sign, scale, currency and period
checks remain active.

Quoted table evidence can use a distinctive literal unit declaration to resolve
competing same-label rows. Row-only ambiguous quotations remain rejected.
Equivalent Chinese shared narrative dates and per-unit wording preserve their
source bindings. Explicit model-authored magnitude annotations can represent a
negative percentage as a loss/expense magnitude; unannotated sign changes remain
invalid. Bounded repair receives these exact source-native percentage options.

Meaning checks allow adjacent quotation chunks only when the original page
proves their continuity. The prompt requires exact source contexts rather than
reconstructed table strings. Accounting signs remain signed endpoints. Wrong
direction, unsupported causal claims, invented context separators and unsupported
rounded amounts remain rejected.

## Source appendix and rendering

Geometry-supported N/A cells and final unlabelled subtotals remain in literal
grids. Wrapped labels to the left of data columns cannot become repeated headers.
Numbered small-print annotations are retained using original font and placement
evidence. They appear on editable pages after their table and are checked for
exact character preservation after save/reload.

Coverage trace pages describe the scope items actually rendered, rather than
unused planned quality-page citations. Long presentations use contiguous local
rendering batches with page-count checks and a 300 MB output limit. The overall
limit is 500 slides; the normal per-worker limit remains 150. Missing batch pages
block export. The final PPT and its content-preservation digest are unchanged by
temporary renderer inputs. No cloud renderer is used.
