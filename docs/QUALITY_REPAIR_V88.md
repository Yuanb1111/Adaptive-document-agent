# Evidence-bound presentation repair (v88)

The repair addresses misplaced audit qualifiers, repeated comparison descriptions,
missing shared scenario assumptions, stale closing copy and interrupted coverage reviews.

- Audit qualifiers are reconciled against the selected observations' explicit period
  metadata. Unknown audit status remains unknown; unrelated caveats are preserved.
  The original wording and corrected wording remain in the validation audit.
- Source comparisons retain their complete rows and use measured column widths at
  readable sizes. Shared assumptions can be restored only from a literal conditional
  clause immediately before the first cited scenario. Its original page citation is
  retained. Continuations do not repeat the whole introductory paragraph.
- Closing findings reuse the final, source-validated model-authored executive briefing.
  Earlier closing drafts remain in speaker notes. An incomplete coverage review is
  visibly disclosed in the closing section.
- A model's inclusion of another source view requires every underlying fact to match
  values, raw units, periods, entity, categories, definitions and audit status. Original
  observation IDs and raw values remain intact, with explicit representation links.
  This check does not decide analytical importance or automatically select topics.
- Large coverage batches allow at most one targeted semantic repair. Complete facts,
  failed attempts and validation errors remain in the audit. Eight batches and the
  existing per-request context bound remain hard limits. Failure preserves the
  original selection and the incomplete-review warning.

These engineering checks do not establish business acceptance. A locally re-exported
saved analysis whose coverage review was not rerun remains pending. No model requests
are needed for the local wording/layout repair. Completion of semantic coverage still
requires the configured model and applicable privacy authorization.

The supplied file is a fix sample. Holdout business acceptance follows
[EVALUATION_PROTOCOL.md](EVALUATION_PROTOCOL.md), including source-supported key facts,
material-omission review and measured human editing time.
