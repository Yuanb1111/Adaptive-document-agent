# Source periods and presentation reference recovery (v98)

The two reviewed downloads were generated by v96, before complete Summary reading
was added. This update preserves v97's complete part-by-part reading, quoted facts,
and explicit include/merge/omit decisions. Source reading, topic selection, scoring
and analytical interpretation retain deep reasoning; the previously approved
lighter brief/outline/layout policy stays in place.

## Corrections

- Borderless PDF headers recognize explicit one-to-twelve-month durations,
  including five-month comparative columns. Coordinate superheaders distinguish
  annual flows, interim flows and `As of` snapshot dates. Ambiguous mixed headers
  remain unresolved. Compact PDF spellings such as `Sixmonthsended` still work.
  Five-month and annual observations therefore no longer authorize growth rates
  or CAGR across unlike durations. Literal raw values and page evidence remain.
- Date ordering uses the actual year, month and day; the day in `31 May 2025` is
  no longer mistaken for a two-digit year. Interim assurance markers survive.
- Topic references missing only their namespace can bind to a unique, complete
  catalog suffix. Partial hashes, unknown references and ambiguous suffixes remain
  invalid. Changes are audited and all numerical/scope validation still applies.
  Initial rejected topics retain their exact observation scopes for later review.
- Coverage notices check the final renderer's observations and existing exact
  fact corroboration rules. Fully represented scopes have no missing-topic notice;
  partially represented scopes are identified as partial, while genuine omissions
  remain visible. A failed draft does not imply absent source evidence.
- Bounded brief repair receives exact currency, signed coefficient and native
  scale from that item's literal quotations and validated table context. It can
  repair the original lead or counterpoint without repeating a rounded conversion.
  Monetary validation is unchanged; hints do not authorize rescaling or invention.
- Literal, uniquely bound row footnotes qualify topic selection and appear beside
  the relevant charts. For example, cash operating measures retain their excluded
  non-cash costs and working-capital adjustments. Ambiguous notes are not attached.
- Full composition headings cannot be replaced by a single colon-qualified
  component. Contents order matches the actual introductory pages. Long cover
  titles use the verified company name and retain the complete title in notes.
  An optional flow page is omitted if a whole heading word cannot fit at the
  template's readable size; source-backed introduction copy and notes are retained.
  Internal excerpt/context comments are excluded from audience copy.

Pipeline, extraction and analysis cache contracts advance to v98, extraction-v5
and analysis-v52. A fresh deployed upload is required to exercise the new extraction
and model responses; previously downloaded JSON is not silently rewritten.

## Validation and limits

Generic regressions cover arbitrary month durations, compact headings, snapshot
precedence, incompatible growth claims, date sorting, strict reference aliases,
partial/complete coverage, literal footnotes and source-native brief repair.
Original PDF pages were also re-extracted locally, verifying annual versus
five-month revenue, operating-cost and turnover headers and exact snapshot dates.
No document content was sent to a provider during these checks.

Both recorded results were replayed through the actual PowerPoint exporter and
local Artifact renderer. All 17 and 22 physical pages passed the visual gate;
critical cover, composition, coverage and cost-footnote pages were inspected.
These replay decks retain the old recorded numeric observations: they verify the
renderer, not a fresh analysis of those documents, and are not replacement outputs.

The first complete Windows run exposed annual-basis and compact-header regressions;
both were fixed and their focused tests pass. Standard Linux CI runs the full suite
and real LibreOffice rendering on Python 3.11 and 3.12. Three locally unavailable
Gemini SDK transport probes require offline tokenizer data and are left to that CI;
no provider or privacy protections were relaxed to accommodate the Windows runner.
The final local suite passed 3,112 tests, with eight opt-in integrations skipped
and the three SDK probes deselected. The latest extraction-focused rerun passed
34 tests after preserving existing FY dimensions and compact month headers.

The observed v96 analysis/export timings were approximately 9m08s and 7m06s for
the two runs, excluding upload/UI time. The new reference binding avoids preventable
invalid-reference recovery, while source page indexes are reused for footnote
retrieval. This update adds no unconditional model call. Complete Summary reading
already overlaps the analytical pipeline with bounded concurrency. Actual upload
to download latency and live editorial quality still require the user's fresh run;
local replay/export timings are not an end-to-end speed claim.
